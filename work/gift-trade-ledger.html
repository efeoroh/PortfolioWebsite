Gift Trade Ledger

A scroll-driven data story and market explorer built on a UK retailer's full year of invoices.

D3.js · Scroll storytelling
Role
Solo build
Stack
Python, pandas, D3.js, JavaScript
Data
UCI Online Retail dataset, 541,909 invoice lines
Period
December 2010 to November 2011
Context

Why it mattered

A UK online gift wholesaler logged every line of every invoice for a year: 541,909 rows from the UCI Online Retail dataset. Transaction logs like this hold a business's real story, but almost nobody reads them.

I built a scroll-driven story on top of it, with animated charts and a world map, followed by an explorer that rewrites its KPIs, charts and executive brief for any market you pick.

The question
What does a year of invoices say about seasonality, customers, markets and concentration risk, and can anyone explore it without help?
  • When does the business make its money?
  • Is growth coming from new or returning customers?
  • How much revenue rides on a few accounts?
Approach

How I solved it

01 · Source

One table, verified.

The full UCI dataset, checked against its published counts: 25,900 invoices, 4,372 customers, 38 country labels.

02 · Clean

Simple, stated rules.

Twelve full months, sales only, merchandise only, aggregated to a small JSON file the page loads instantly.

03 · Story

The chart follows the reader.

As each paragraph scrolls into view, the sticky chart beside it changes: seasonal bars, new against returning customers, a world map, a concentration curve.

04 · Explain

A brief for any market.

Pick a market and the brief compares it with the whole business and labels what matters.

Outcome

What it delivers

£9.66Mmerchandise sales across 12 months
37%of sales landed September to November
60%of revenue from the top 10% of customers
36markets to explore, each with its own brief
