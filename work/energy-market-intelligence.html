Energy Market Intelligence Dashboard

Four Statistics Canada energy indicators in one Power BI model, with a monthly executive summary written automatically for each province.

Context

Why it mattered

Electricity prices, natural gas demand, the generation mix and energy inflation move together, but Statistics Canada publishes them as separate tables with different units and release timing. Reading them side by side for one province meant opening four tables and doing the arithmetic by hand.

This dashboard pulls all four through the Statistics Canada API into one model, switches between Canada and the provinces in a click, splits large and small electricity consumers, and writes a short executive summary of what changed this month.

The question
For a chosen province and month, what changed in power prices, gas demand, low-emission supply and energy inflation, and what does it mean?
  • Are electricity prices rising for large or small consumers?
  • Is natural gas demand spiking, and why?
  • Is low-emission generation gaining share?
Approach

How I solved it

01 · Ingest

Four tables through one API.

A Python pipeline requests each table from the Statistics Canada REST endpoints, so a new release needs no downloads.

02 · Align

Put four tables on one calendar.

Dates, units and geography are harmonised so an index, gigajoules and a percentage share can sit on the same monthly axis.

03 · Model

Shared dimensions, four measures.

Every indicator joins to the same date and geography tables, plus consumer class for prices and technology for generation.

04 · Measure

One glance per province.

Each KPI shows the latest month, the previous month and the change, with a trend behind it. These are Alberta's values from the demo.

05 · Explain

Numbers in, sentences out.

Measures read the direction and size of each change and write four short notes: market insight, demand signal, supply outlook and economic impact.

06 · Refresh

New release, new report.

The pull, clean and load steps run on a schedule, and the report refreshes when the data does.
