Industry Profiles Automation

All 18 annual industry profiles rebuilt from Statistics Canada data in a single run.

Automation · R
Role
Built the automation
Stack
R, Statistics Canada tables, Excel outputs
Data
Labour Force Survey, establishment size, business counts, wages
Scale
2M+ rows, 18 industries
Context

Why it mattered

Alberta publishes annual profiles of its major industries, from mining and oil and gas extraction to health care. Every profile carries the same tables: labour force characteristics, working conditions, employment by establishment size, business counts, gender, age and wages.

Building them meant querying Statistics Canada tables by hand for each of 18 industries and formatting dozens of Excel tables. It took about a month each cycle, and small inconsistencies crept in between profiles.

The question
Can all 18 industry profiles be refreshed for a new reporting year in a single run, with identical structure every time?
  • Which tables does each profile need, and from where?
  • How do we make every industry follow the same rules?
  • How do analysts get publication-ready files without copy and paste?
Approach

How I solved it

01 · Run

Pick a year, press run.

A small app takes the reporting year and runs the whole pipeline, ticking off each table as it is written.

02 · Pull

Every source in one pass.

The pipeline requests each Statistics Canada table once for all industries, more than 2 million rows.

03 · Process

Same rules for every industry.

Filters, aggregations and shares are written once, so every profile is calculated the same way.

04 · Loop

One template, 126 outputs.

The template runs for each of the 18 industries and writes all seven outputs for each one.

05 · Deliver

Ready for the profile document.

Excel files land in a folder, one per table, already in publication format.

Outcome

What it delivers

126outputs per run: 18 industries × 7 tables and charts
2M+rows processed
1 runinstead of about a month of manual work
95%+less time on each annual cycle
